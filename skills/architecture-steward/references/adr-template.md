# ADR Template

## Title

Use a short action-oriented title.

## Status

Proposed, accepted, superseded, or deprecated.

## Context

- What problem or pressure led to this decision?
- What constraints matter?
- What assumptions are being made?

## Decision

State the chosen architecture move clearly.

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected
- Costs and tradeoffs accepted
- New risks introduced

## Alternatives Considered

List the main options rejected and why.

## Follow-up

- Migration steps
- Validation plan
- Conditions that should trigger revisiting this ADR
